EXERCISE 03 / AFTER YOUR ATTEMPT

One defensible decision

A prepared example using fictional evidence. This is not an answer key for every reasonable investigation, or a verified service finding.

Initial judgment

Investigate. Provisional category: test synchronization. CI-01 establishes that the confirmation element was absent at 1,002 ms. CODE-01 uses a fixed 1,000 ms sleep and an immediate snapshot. HIST-01 shows an association between confirmation timing and the test outcome. None establishes global service instability.

Two competing hypotheses

  1. Preferred: the snapshot happens before a valid confirmation. CODE-01 supplies the mechanism; HIST-01 shows failures when confirmation is after the snapshot.
  2. Alternative: the reset genuinely fails or breaches its timing contract in some runs. A failed assertion motivates checking this possibility, but does not establish it. The success-looking history timings weaken a blanket failure explanation, but the raw correlated outcome and the expected timing bound are still needed.

Contradicting evidence: no direct observation disproving the timing-race hypothesis is supplied. The historical timing association supports it but does not establish the cause of timing variation.

Would weaken the preferred hypothesis: a correctly correlated capture showing a reset error, confirmation already present before the failed snapshot, or confirmation beyond the accepted timing contract. These are proposed discriminators, not observed events.

One next action

Request TIM-01 to identify the acceptable completion bound before judging the supplied timings. Missing evidence still includes the correlated run 204 response/confirmation capture and why timing varies. The initial absence of an element is not itself a timeout breach.

After the request

TIM-01 is revealed. Its 3,000 ms bound makes the history's supplied confirmation timings compatible with the narrow integration expectation, while still requiring a successful reset response. Keep the decision as investigate: the raw correlated capture has not been read, and the sample cannot establish overall health or the cause of latency. This does not establish the new/old-password authentication outcomes in AC-04.

Other defensible choices

Reject the certainty: “The supplied evidence does not establish service instability; I retain it as a hypothesis.” This does not mean “the service is healthy.”

Request TRACE-01: correlate the response and confirmation with run 204/request reset-204. Record the request as pending for Exercise 6; no trace is revealed here. If you already read TIM-01 in Exercise 2, cite it as prior knowledge. A pending evidence request is a valid outcome.

Alternative category: runtime variation or evidence correlation may deserve investigation when you explain what observation would discriminate it from a timing race. No category alone confirms a root cause.

Common reasoning problems

  • HIST-01 copies the underlying event data; counting it as independent corroboration exaggerates confidence.
  • The separate password-policy probe did not cause the recorded reset_completion failure.
  • Extending a sleep immediately may hide a timing problem. A rerun without a question may produce another result without explaining it. Neither action is executed here.
  • Confirmation alone does not test every acceptance criterion. A timing association is not proof of a healthy service.

Self-review

Can another tester distinguish your observations from your hypotheses? Do both hypotheses remain open to evidence? Is your proposed next observation relevant and correctly correlated? Is your conclusion limited to what you actually read?
