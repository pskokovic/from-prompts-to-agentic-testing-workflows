          How scoring works
          Each dimension is scored out of 100 using fixed weights, then deductions are applied and scores are clamped to 0–100. Roles receive equal credit. Free text receives no credit.
          • Clarity: role 20, specific task 50, functional scope 30.
          • Grounding: requirement 40, acceptance criteria 60.
          • Output Control: table 50, coverage summary 25, source references 25.
          • Hallucination Resistance: no invention 40, explicit assumptions 30, source references with at least one supplied source 30.
          Conflicting instructions lose the credits they override. Earlier-release authority: Grounding −45 and Hallucination Resistance −20. Gap-filling as fact: Resistance −30, plus Clarity −20 when paired with no invention. Hiding gaps: Resistance −20, plus Clarity −15 when paired with assumptions or questions. Summary-only: Output Control −20, plus Clarity −15 when paired with tables or references. A heading earns no points.
          For teaching purposes, the simulation follows the harmful instruction when choices conflict. Real AI systems may resolve conflicts differently.
          Average score: below 40 = Limited; 40–74 = Developing; 75–100 = Strong structure. A high score still requires human review.
