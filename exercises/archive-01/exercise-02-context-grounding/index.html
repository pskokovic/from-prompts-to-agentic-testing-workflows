01 / UNDERSTAND THE TASK

Build a defensible context package

Your team will ask an AI assistant to propose functional password-reset tests. Choose which sources to include, exclude, or defer for later work. You are selecting evidence; CI diagnosis comes later.

THE FIXED REQUEST

Derive reviewable functional password-reset test conditions and expected results. Cite supplied source IDs. Do not invent requirements; separate supported expectations from open questions.

Your finished package

  • Sources selected for this task.
  • Two reasons for excluding or deferring sources.
  • An explanation of the expiry conflict.
  • Two unresolved requirements questions.

12 minutes: 2 instruction · 6 work · 3 review · 1 transition. Work individually or in pairs. Aim for a justified package, not the most sources.

02 / SELECT THE EVIDENCE

Choose what belongs

Include = supply now. Exclude = leave out of this task. Defer = keep for a later question. Inspect each source's origin and scope before deciding.

Resolve the conflict and retain the gaps

Questions should identify missing expected behaviour, not silently fill it in.
