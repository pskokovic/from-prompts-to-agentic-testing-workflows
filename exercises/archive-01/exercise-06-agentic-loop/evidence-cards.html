VALID-01 · approval required
This card represents a simulated execution, never a real CI call. Ask the participant to record approval before revealing it.

With isolated fixtures and controlled confirmation delays of 700 ms and 1,460 ms, the original snapshot test passes the first and fails the second. A proposed test that waits for a successful confirmation with a maximum of 3,000 ms passes both. It fails at 3,000 ms when confirmation is withheld. Separate fresh-session checks establish new-password success and old-password rejection in both successful comparison cases. A deliberately injected old-password-still-valid fault is detected by the stronger assertions, while the original confirmation-only assertion passes it.

These authored examples illustrate discriminating validation, not a verified patch. Recommend implementing and validating the change against the actual system before accepting it. Do not claim all intermittent failures are fixed.
