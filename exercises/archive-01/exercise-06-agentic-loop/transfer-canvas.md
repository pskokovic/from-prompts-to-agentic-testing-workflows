## Compact worked example

Investigate run 204, not global service health. Use CI-01/CODE-01/TIM-01 and a source-linked ledger; request matching trace before a cause recommendation. Read is allowed; isolated comparison needs scoped approval. Preserve hypotheses, identities, observations, and action count. With a budget of three, stop for review when correlated evidence supports a limited recommendation; escalate missing identity or stop unresolved at the cap. The automation reviewer checks citations and whether contrary evidence would change the action. Success means a reviewable, bounded handoff, not a green simulation.
